FAQ

Common questions answered

Everything you need to know about selling your property directly to Properties714.

How fast can you close?
Once we reach an agreement, we work to close as quickly as the situation allows, coordinated through a licensed title company. Timelines vary by property — we'll give you a realistic estimate up front, and you help choose the closing date.
Do I need to make repairs before selling?
No. We buy properties as-is, in any condition. Any repairs or improvements happen on our side after closing — not yours.
Are there any fees or commissions?
When you sell directly to us, there's no agent commission to pay. We'll always be upfront about any costs specific to your transaction before you agree to anything.
How is an offer calculated?
We look at the property's current condition, location, comparable sales in the area, and the cost of any needed repairs, then present a number we can explain and stand behind.
What if I'm facing foreclosure?
Timing matters. We're not able to provide legal advice, but we can look at your property and situation honestly and tell you what may be possible — reach out through the form as soon as you can.
